Amharic

Notes

Amharic is one of the most widely spoken languages in Ethiopia. It is used in schools, homes, government, and daily communication. Learning Amharic helps students read, write, speak, and understand their language more clearly.

In Amharic, vocabulary, grammar, reading, and speaking practice are all important. Students learn new words, understand sentence structure, and improve their ability to communicate in daily life and classroom settings.

  • Vocabulary
  • Sentence practice
  • Reading and dialogue
  • Speaking and listening

Key concepts

  • Learning new words helps improve speaking and writing.
  • Greeting people respectfully is an important part of communication.
  • Simple sentences help us ask questions and share ideas clearly.
  • Practice with dialogue improves listening and speaking skills.

Worked example

Example dialogue: “ሰላም!” “ሰላም! እንደምን ነዎት?” “እንደምን ነኝ፣ እጅግ ደህና ነኝ።” This conversation shows a greeting and a polite question about a person’s well-being.

Practice questions

  1. What does “ሰላም” mean?
  2. What does “ቤት” mean in English?
  3. What is the Amharic word for “teacher”?
  4. What is the Amharic word for “water”?
  5. What does “አመሰግናለሁ” mean?
