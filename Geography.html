Geography

What is geography?

Geography is the study of places, people, environments, and the relationships between them. It helps us understand where things are and how physical and human features shape everyday life.

Maps and location

Maps represent places and help us find locations. A map key explains symbols, a scale compares map distance with real distance, and directions such as north, south, east, and west help describe location.

Physical and human features

Mountains, rivers, plains, and climate are physical features. Roads, farms, towns, and settlements are human features. People depend on and change their environments, so responsible use of resources matters.

Key ideas

  • Physical geography studies natural features and processes.
  • Human geography studies people, places, and activities.
  • Maps use symbols and scale to communicate information about places.

Practice

Look at a map of your area. Use its key to identify two features, then describe where one is in relation to the other using compass directions.
