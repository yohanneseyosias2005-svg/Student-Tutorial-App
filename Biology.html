Biology

Living things and cells

Biology is the study of living things. All living organisms are made of one or more cells. Cells are the basic units of life and carry out processes that help organisms grow, use energy, and respond to their surroundings.

Organisms and life processes

Living organisms need materials and energy. Many plants make sugars through photosynthesis, using light, water, and carbon dioxide. Animals obtain energy by consuming food. Organisms also grow, reproduce, and respond to changes in their environments.

Ecosystems

An ecosystem includes living organisms and the non-living parts of their environment. Producers, such as green plants, make food; consumers obtain energy by eating other organisms; and decomposers break down dead material and return nutrients to the environment.

Key ideas

  • Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms.
  • Food chains show how energy moves between organisms.
  • Biodiversity is the variety of living things in an area.
