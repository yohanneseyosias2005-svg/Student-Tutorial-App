English

Notes

English is a very important language because it is used in reading, writing, speaking, and understanding many kinds of information. It helps students learn new ideas, communicate with others, and succeed in school and daily life. Good English skills make learning easier in many subjects.

English has grammar, vocabulary, spelling, reading, and writing. When students practice these skills, they become better at expressing ideas clearly and confidently. Learning English also helps people understand books, stories, conversations, and information from different places.

  • Grammar
  • Vocabulary
  • Reading comprehension
  • Writing and speaking

Key concepts

  • A sentence must have a subject and a verb.
  • Nouns name a person, place, or thing.
  • Adjectives describe nouns.
  • Verbs show action or state.

Worked example

Example: “The dog runs fast.” Here, “dog” is the noun, “runs” is the verb, and “fast” is an adverb that tells how the action happens. This sentence is clear and complete.

Practice questions

  1. Choose the correct sentence.
  2. Find the noun in the sentence: “The teacher writes on the board.”
  3. What is the opposite of “small”?
  4. Which word is a verb: jump, chair, blue?
  5. What is the plural of “child”?
