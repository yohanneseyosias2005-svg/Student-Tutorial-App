Citizenship

Notes

Citizenship teaches us how to be responsible members of our community and country. Good citizens respect the law, help others, protect the environment, and act with honesty and fairness. Citizenship is about being a positive example and contributing to the common good.

People who are good citizens obey rules, take part in community activities, and care for public places. They also respect the rights and dignity of others. These values help society become peaceful, safe, and united.

  • Respect for the law
  • Helping others
  • Caring for the environment
  • Being honest and fair
  • Taking part in community activities

Key concepts

  • Good citizens respect the law and the rights of others.
  • Honesty and fairness help people trust one another.
  • Community participation makes society stronger and cleaner.
  • Protecting the environment is a responsibility for everyone.

Worked example

Example: A student sees litter in the school compound. Instead of ignoring it, the student picks up the rubbish and tells classmates to keep the environment clean. This action shows responsibility, respect for others, and care for the community.

Practice questions

  1. Why is it important to respect the law?
  2. What does honesty mean?
  3. How can a person help the environment?
  4. Why is voting important?
  5. What is one way to build peace in a community?
