Chemistry

Matter and its properties

Chemistry studies matter, the materials around us, and how they change. Matter has mass and takes up space. It commonly exists as a solid, liquid, or gas; heating and cooling can change its state.

Elements, compounds, and mixtures

An element is made of one kind of atom. A compound contains atoms of different elements chemically joined in a fixed proportion. A mixture contains substances together without forming a new compound, so its parts may often be separated by physical methods.

Physical and chemical changes

A physical change alters a material's form or state without making a new substance. A chemical change produces one or more new substances. Observations such as a lasting color change or gas formation can be clues, but should be interpreted carefully.

Key ideas

  • Atoms are the small building blocks that make up elements.
  • Compounds have different properties from the elements that form them.
  • In a mixture, each substance keeps its identity.
