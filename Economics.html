Economics

What is economics?

Economics studies how people and societies make choices about using limited resources to meet their needs and wants. Because resources are limited, choosing one option often means giving up another.

Needs, wants, and resources

Needs are essential for life, such as food, water, and shelter. Wants are things people would like to have. Resources include time, skills, land, materials, and money; careful planning helps people decide how best to use them.

Goods, services, and markets

Goods are physical items people can use, while services are useful tasks performed for others. In a market, buyers and sellers exchange goods or services. Prices can be affected by how much of an item is available and how many people want it.

Key ideas

  • Scarcity means resources are limited compared with people’s wants.
  • Opportunity cost is the next-best option given up when making a choice.
  • Saving a portion of income can help people plan for future needs.
