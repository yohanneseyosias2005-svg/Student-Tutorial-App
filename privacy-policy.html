Privacy policy

Student Tutorial App Demo

This demo stores information you enter, such as email address, phone number, grade, subject, lesson requests, and chat messages, locally on your device. It does not send this information to a Student Tutorial App server. The demo has no shared accounts, real tutor matching, or payment processing.

If you choose to continue by SMS, the app opens your device's messaging app with a prepared message. You review and send it yourself. Your phone or messaging provider may process that message under its own privacy policy. Telegram links open Telegram or your browser; those services have their own privacy policies.

The optional camera preview requests camera access only when you start it. Video is shown locally in the app and is not recorded or sent to another person.

Signing out ends the current session but does not delete saved demo data. To remove locally stored information, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. This demo does not provide a server account or server-side account deletion.

Do not enter sensitive information or use this demo to make payments. Payments and teacher payouts are not enabled.
