History

What is history?

History is the study of people and events from the past. It helps us understand how communities and countries have changed over time, and how earlier decisions and experiences shape life today.

Sources of historical information

Historians learn from sources such as letters, photographs, tools, buildings, oral accounts, and written records. Comparing sources can help us understand an event more carefully and notice that accounts may differ.

Timelines and change

A timeline places events in chronological order. Dates and timelines help us compare events, identify patterns, and see what changed or continued over a period.

Key ideas

  • History studies people, societies, and events in the past.
  • Primary sources come from the time being studied; other sources interpret or describe it later.
  • Reliable historical explanations use evidence and consider different perspectives.

Practice

Choose an old photograph from your community. Ask who made it, when it was made, and what other evidence could help explain what it shows.
