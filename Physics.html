Physics

Forces and motion

Physics helps explain how objects move and interact. A force is a push or pull. An unbalanced force can change an object's speed or direction, while balanced forces do not change its motion.

Energy

Energy is the ability to cause change. Common forms include movement (kinetic), stored (potential), heat, light, and sound energy. Energy can change from one form to another, while the total amount is conserved in a closed system.

Simple electric circuits

An electric circuit is a complete path through which electric charge can flow. A simple circuit may include a source, wires, and a load such as a lamp. An open circuit has a break in its path, so current does not flow.

Key ideas

  • Speed describes distance traveled per unit of time.
  • Friction is a force that opposes sliding between surfaces.
  • Electrical safety matters: never handle exposed wires or mains electricity.
